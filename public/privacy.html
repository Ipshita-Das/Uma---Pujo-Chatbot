Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Uma is a free chat assistant for Durga Puja: pandals, adda, food, rituals and outfits. This page explains what information Uma keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile photo link and Google's account ID. Uma asks Google for nothing else and never sees your Google password.
  • Your chats: the text of the messages you send and Uma's replies, so you can come back to them.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in on your device.

Photos

If you share a photo (for example, for outfit ideas), it is sent to the AI service only to answer that message. Uma does not save your photos. After you reload the page, the photo is gone, and the chat only notes that a photo was shared.

How your information is used

  • To sign you in, greet you by name, and show your saved chats.
  • To answer your questions. Your recent messages (and any photo you just shared) are sent to the AI service that writes the reply.

Uma does not show ads, and does not sell or share your information for marketing.

Services Uma relies on

  • Google: sign-in, and the Gemini AI service for some replies.
  • Groq: the AI service that writes most replies.
  • Render: hosts the website and server.
  • Turso: stores accounts and chats.

These services process data on Uma's behalf under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete any chat yourself from the chat menu (☰ → the bin icon). It is removed straight away.
  • To delete your whole account and all your chats, email the address below from the Gmail you sign in with.
  • Signing out removes your sign-in cookie. Sign-in sessions otherwise expire after 30 days at most.

Children

Uma is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
